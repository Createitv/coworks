Anthropic lists Cowork for eligible paid Claude plans. Claude Free has its own chat and tool features. Cowork access depends on your plan, device, and workspace settings. Pricing and platform references checked November 12, 2026. Check the [official pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) for the current subscription cost in your region and the [availability guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15520349-use-claude-cowork-on-web-desktop-and-mobile) for your plan and device.

| What you want to do | What to check before choosing a plan |
| --- | --- |
| Try a cloud task on the web | Check web availability for your paid plan and account |
| Organize files from your computer | Check that your operating system supports Claude Desktop and that it can connect the working folder |
| Continue a task from your phone | Check mobile availability and whether the session needs an open desktop connection |
| Use Cowork with a work account | Ask the workspace administrator about Cowork access and connector policies |
| Run recurring reports | Check scheduling support, source permissions, and your expected usage |

## A practical way to compare plans

Write down the tasks you expect to run each week. Identify which require local files or work connectors, then try one representative task. Record how much time you spend reviewing its result and whether the plan covers the account and device you actually use. Compare those observations with the current billing terms before upgrading.

If you already have a paid account but cannot find the feature, use the [missing Cowork tab checklist](/docs/setup#missing-cowork).
