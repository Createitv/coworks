## Download a three-file exercise

[Download the synthetic research pack](/docs/downloads/research-brief.zip). The fictional question is whether to expand an internal pilot. The pack contains an early survey, a later eligibility audit, and a rollout note. Unzip it into a new folder and use copies for the exercise.

## Run the brief

> Read the three source files. Write evidence.md with columns for claim, source filename, source date, metric or population, and caveat. Preserve conflicting numbers as separate rows with their context. Then draft brief.md with a recommendation, supporting evidence, and open questions. Do not make changes to the sources. If the files do not establish a fact, say so.

## Check the answer

| Check | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Distinguish the two counts | 42 survey replies and 36 eligible employees use different denominators and are not interchangeable |
| Calculate the audited outcome | 27 of 36 eligible employees completed the pilot, or 75% |
| Attribute the threshold | The rollout note calls for at least 80% completion and evidence from a second team |
| Identify the missing evidence | The files give no completion data for the second team |
| Recommend a next step | Keep the current pilot and collect the missing evidence before recommending expansion |

Open all cited files and check the source dates. A brief that recommends expansion based on survey replies has used the wrong population. For another workflow with a fixed answer key, try the [receipt audit](/docs/receipt-audit).
